Privacy policy

Edz Central and the Life Record Android app · Effective 6 October 2026

Edz Central (including its Android app, Life Record) is a personal project run by Edwin Roosevelt. This policy explains what data it collects, how it uses Google user data, and how you can remove it.

Data you enter

Everything you add yourself: diary entries, notes, photos and files, trips, transactions, accounts and the bank SMS messages the app forwards. It is stored so the app can show it back to you, and is used for nothing else.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, Edz Central receives your name, email address and Google account ID. They are used only to create your account and sign you in.

Gmail data

Connecting Gmail is optional. When you connect it, Edz Central asks for read-only access (gmail.readonly) and your email address. It never sends, deletes, labels or changes your mail. It uses this access for two things only:

  • Bank alerts. If you list your bank's sender addresses, Edz Central reads emails from those senders to record the transactions they describe. It keeps the alert's text with the transaction.
  • Receipt details. When a payment is recorded, Edz Central searches your mail from around the time of that payment, leaving out the Promotions and Social categories. It reads the emails it finds to decide which one is the merchant's receipt or booking confirmation. From the matching email it keeps the description and line items, the order or booking number, the sender, subject, date and message ID, and the names of its attachments. It also keeps the IDs of emails it has already checked, so they are not read again. It does not keep the full text of the emails it reads. An attachment is copied into your records only when you choose to import it.

A refresh token is stored on the server so this can happen in the background. It is never shown to the app or to anyone else.

Who processes your data

  • Anthropic (Claude API). When an email has no built-in reader, its sender, subject and text are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to extract the receipt details. Anthropic processes it to return the result and does not use API data to train its models.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores the database, and Amazon Web Services (S3) stores files.

Your data is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with anyone else, except where the law requires it. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models. No person reads your email unless you ask for help with a specific message, or it is needed for security or to comply with the law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Edz Central's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data is kept until you delete it or ask for it to be deleted.
  • Disconnecting Gmail in the app (Settings → Gmail → Disconnect) revokes Edz Central's access and deletes the stored token. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
  • To delete your account and everything stored with it, including details taken from your email, email the address below. It will be done within 30 days.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to the servers and database is limited to the developer.
